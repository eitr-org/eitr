# Information, media and notifications

Notification cards show their local arrival date and time beneath the title,
in both popups and notification history. Times are kept only for this session.

## System > Information

**About** displays the EITR summary, including the installed EITR version, in a terminal. **Hardware** shows CPU, GPU,
RAM and storage for the drive backing `/`; other disks are not combined into
that total. Missing sensor/GPU telemetry is marked unavailable.

**AI Usage** detects installed/configured clients. Cached usage appears first;
refresh does not launch sign-in or conversations. It shows each account's
limits; percentages mean the part of a limit already used.

**Weather** uses the default location and supports temporary city searches.
The override ends when the panel closes. Escape clears city search before
closing; temperature units follow the location, with an alternate unit alongside.

## Calendar and media

Click the clock/date for the calendar. It keeps six week rows and shows year
progress below the grid. Navigate with the previous/next buttons, Today, arrow
keys or T. Search Calendar from Walker if the bar is hidden.

Click the animated equalizer for cover/title/artist, previous/play/next and
seeking when supported. It hides without an eligible player and excludes
recognized meetings. The bars indicate playback, not captured audio.

## Notifications

**SUPER + N** or the bell opens the notification center. Notifications group by
application. The newest card fronts a stack; expand it to see older cards.
Dismiss one card or clear a group/all deliberately. Popups also stack by app,
showing the newest message over backing layers; hover pauses their expiry.

Notifications have a soft inward glow in the theme's accent colour; critical
ones glow in the theme's red instead, and low-priority ones are subdued with
no glow. Do Not Disturb suppresses popups
without deleting history. History stays in memory across config reloads, not
process restarts. No notification contents are saved as a log.
