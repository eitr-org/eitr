# Information, media and notifications

Notification cards show their local arrival date and time beneath the title,
in both popups and notification history. Times are kept only for this session.

## Read system information

Open Settings > About for the Eitr version, codename, update channel, kernel and licence, and this PC's processor, graphics, motherboard, installed memory and every connected drive with its capacity. The System overview shows your profile, current hardware usage and network speed (download and upload). For detailed hardware, AI Usage and weather, click their bar icons; Walker also finds them by name.

In Settings > About, choose Emblem or Runes under About logo to change the terminal About page. The choice stays on this machine and defaults to Emblem; both follow the current theme accent.

**Hardware** shows CPU, GPU,
RAM and storage for the drive backing `/`; other disks are not combined into
that total. Missing sensor/GPU telemetry is marked unavailable.

**AI Usage** detects installed/configured clients. Cached usage appears first;
refresh does not launch sign-in or conversations. It shows each account's
limits; percentages mean the part of a limit already used.

Every detected provider has its own section with its plan, limits and errors. Click a heading to fold or unfold only that section. Sections start closed at login and keep their open or closed state for the session. Long lists scroll within the screen.

**Weather** uses the default location and supports temporary city searches.
The override ends when the panel closes. Escape clears city search before
closing; temperature units follow the location, with an alternate unit alongside.

## Calendar and media

Click the clock/date for the calendar. It keeps six week rows and shows year
progress below the grid. Navigate with the previous/next buttons, Today, arrow
keys or T. Search Calendar from Walker if the bar is hidden. Click a day for its
reminders below the grid; the + beside the day opens its plans in place, with a field to
add a reminder at a time that day, and the back arrow returns to the month.

Click the animated equalizer for the Media panel: the cover ringed by bars that follow
the music, the song, its lyrics, previous/play/next and seeking when supported. It hides
without an eligible player and excludes recognized meetings. The bar's equalizer follows
live system audio through a private cava process only while playback and the strip are
visible. Drag the equalizer to the left to swap it for the line being sung; drag the line
either way to swap it back; pausing or changing song keeps it. Online lyrics are a Desktop Bar setting (see
the Desktop bar chapter).

## Notifications

**SUPER + N** or the bell opens the notification center. Notifications group by
application. The newest card fronts a stack; expand it to see older cards. Dismiss one card or clear a group/all deliberately. Popups also stack by app,
showing the newest message over backing layers; hover pauses their expiry.

**SUPER + Alt + N** runs the newest notification's main action (what clicking it
does), and **SUPER + Alt + Shift + N** dismisses it. A repeated notification (same
app, title and text) replaces the earlier one and shows a count such as ×3 instead
of stacking another card.

Clicking a notification runs its main action. One without an action opens the app that sent it: its window, or for herdr the terminal running herdr, switched to the workspace and tab the notification names. Each card shows the app's own icon when it has one, else the bell.

Use Settings > Notifications for Do Not Disturb and Crash Notifications.
