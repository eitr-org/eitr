# Eitr category artwork

`dark.png` and `light.png` retain the Dark/Light illustrations. The theme
carousel uses theme-specific previews and Sun/Moon filter buttons. These
illustrations and the older JPG exports remain available as artwork.

- Dark: Hati resting beneath a blue full moon, with matching blue glowing eyes.
- Light: Sköll resting beneath a fiery orange sun, with matching orange eyes.

Both show the complete resting wolf in ash ivory, slate and charcoal on opaque
black, without scenery. The owner approved this interpretation of the Ashen Stag
wallpaper’s restrained drawing style. The celestial discs have richer surface
texture and glow.

The built-in image generator created the originals from written briefs, then
revised Eitr’s own drafts to an opaque black background. No third-party image
was attached. Exact original and revision prompts, native sizes and SHA-256
hashes are in `branding/sources/theme-carousel/artwork.json`. The PNGs are the retained native masters (1672×941).
