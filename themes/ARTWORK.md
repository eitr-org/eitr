# Wallpaper Collection

The built-in collection spans 13 themes. The 14 extra themes and their artwork
are maintained in `eitr-org/eitr-themes`; their generation records here are
marked removed with the official repository location. The Eitr theme combines procedural and generated illustration (see themes/eitr/artwork.md). Batches 01-03 originally generated 95 images and the Witcher batch four more, 99 in all. The owner's curation removes and reorders wallpapers; wallpaper names remain stable during review, and manifest entries for removed wallpapers are marked removed with their prompts kept as history.


## Files

- `<theme>/backgrounds/`: installed wallpapers; 3840 x 2160 PNG/JPEG exports.
- `<theme>/artwork.md`: theme-specific prompts and continuation notes.
- `artwork-batch.json`: collection manifest and generation/edit prompts.
- `artwork-batch-02.json`: completed reference-led batch and its generation prompts.
- `artwork-batch-04.json`: owner-approved fresh written-brief replacements, native sources and exact prompts; no image references.
- `artwork-batch-05.json`: the ten approved realm illustrations and their native sources, exact prompts and preview selections.
- `artwork-batch-06.json`: the ten second realm wallpapers, using owner-requested scenic painterly environments, with native sources, exact prompts and framing.
- `artwork-batch-07.json`: realm wallpapers with individually recorded rendering styles, written prompts, native sources and framing; Ragnarök excluded.
- `artwork-batch-13.json`: owner-approved Svartalfheim Feasting Hall, verbatim generation/edit prompts, style input, native sources and export hashes.
- `artwork-batch-12.json`: owner-approved Aurora and Dusk Fjords, verbatim prompts, image input filenames, native sources and export hashes.
- `artwork-batch-11.json`: active rune seals and light Gates; removed dark Gates retained as provenance.
- `artwork-batch-08.json`: three owner-approved Ragnarök selections with separate panoramic compositions, exact written prompts and native provenance.
- `artwork-batch-03.json`: second reference-led round, two artworks per theme, generated with the built-in image tool.
- `artwork-witcher.json`: the four Witcher fan-art wallpapers once made for One Dark Pro, all removed, with their prompts kept as history.


## Resolution

The shipped collection targets 3840×2160 (16:9). Existing raster artwork is cropped to fill while preserving its main subject, then Lanczos-resampled. These are 4K exports rather than claims of native generated detail. Per-file source sizes, hashes and framing are in `artwork-4k.json`; `tools/export-wallpapers-4k.py` repeats the conversion without restoring deleted files. The Eitr runestone and its motion layers are regenerated natively by their source script. Each wallpaper also has a 21:9 version in `backgrounds-ultrawide/` (3440×1440 JPEG), which monitors at least twice as wide as tall show instead; most are the 21:9 originals the 4K files were cropped from. Their sources are in `artwork-ultrawide.json`, and `tools/export-wallpapers-ultrawide.py` rebuilds them. Batch 04 retains its native sources under `branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-04/`; its `export.py` uses both existing exporters. Ultrawide exports use separate panoramic expansions where needed, otherwise subject-centered crops, without stretched edge padding. Per-image framing lives in batch04 and the ultrawide manifest.

## Reference Rights

Batches 02 and 03 were generated with contact sheets of earlier third-party wallpapers attached as mood references; their `reference` fields name the folders those sheets came from. Those wallpapers are no longer in the repository, and every image that came out too close to them was replaced by batch 04. Each theme's icon, VS Code, Neovim and btop files are generated by `build-theme-files.py` from `integrations.json` and the theme's `colors.toml`; run it (or its `--check`) after changing either.

Batch 04 replaces all selected reference-led images that were too close to earlier artwork with fresh illustrations generated from written briefs only: three for Afterglow plus the extra themes now maintained in `eitr-org/eitr-themes`. Their earlier prompts remain marked removed in batches 02/03. Afterglow retains its owner-requested soft painterly rendering with Norse subjects.

Theme previews (`preview.png`) are screenshots of each theme's desktop with Neovim and About open, retaken whenever a theme's look changes; no export script writes them. Batch 04's `previews` list only selects the website's theme-card artwork.

The Catppuccin additions use owner-requested animated-film atmosphere rather
than the collection’s default Norse screenprint direction. Their exact prompts
and native sources live in each theme’s `references/` directory.

## Realm environments — Batch 06

and rich painterly concept-art lighting instead of the default ink screenprint
style and no-scenery rule. This exception applies only to the ten Batch 06
images, in both aspect ratios. The environments follow each theme's
`colors.toml`, with calm space at the top for the desktop bar.

`artwork-batch-06.json` records exact written briefs, source dimensions, hashes
and framing. No reference images were supplied. All architecture and compositions
are original, without characters, readable lettering or third-party designs.
Native masters and `export.py` live in
`branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-06/`. The exporter updates the 4K and ultrawide
manifests without rebuilding previews. Separate panoramic compositions preserve
landmarks where a crop would lose them; exports never stretch or pad images.

`branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-07/`; exports are 3840×2160 and 3440×1440
JPEGs, resampled from the recorded native sizes rather than native 4K detail.
Seven separately composed panoramic masters preserve subjects where a crop
would lose them; other ultrawides use recorded subject-preserving crops.
The soul gate uses its panoramic master for both exports. No stretching or
padding is used. Selected artwork has passed agent visual inspection and
remains available for owner review.

are 3840×2160 and 3440×1440, without stretching or padding; not native 4K detail.
Independent theme previews remain unchanged; website cards use each theme's first wallpaper.
