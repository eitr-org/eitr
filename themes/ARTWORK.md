# Wallpaper Collection

Eitr and Eitr Light are the built-in themes. The [official Store](https://github.com/eitr-org/eitr-themes/tree/main/sources/eitr-realms) owns Afterglow and the realm theme wallpapers, masters, and export recipes. Eitr combines procedural and generated illustration. See [the Eitr artwork notes](eitr/artwork.md).


## Files

- `<theme>/backgrounds/`: installed wallpapers; 3840 x 2160 PNG/JPEG exports.
- `<theme>/artwork.md`: theme-specific prompts and continuation notes.
- `artwork-batch.json`: collection manifest and generation/edit prompts.
- `artwork-batch-02.json`: completed reference-led batch and its generation prompts.
- `artwork-batch-04.json`: owner-approved fresh written-brief replacements, native sources and exact prompts; no image references.
- `artwork-batch-12.json`: owner-approved Aurora and Dusk Fjords, verbatim prompts, image input filenames, native sources and export hashes.
- `artwork-batch-11.json`: active rune seals and light Gates; removed dark Gates retained as provenance.
- `artwork-batch-03.json`: second reference-led round, two artworks per theme, generated with the built-in image tool.
- `artwork-witcher.json`: the four Witcher fan-art wallpapers once made for One Dark Pro, all removed, with their prompts kept as history.


## Resolution

The shipped collection targets 3840×2160 (16:9). Existing raster artwork is cropped to fill while preserving its main subject, then Lanczos-resampled. These are 4K exports rather than claims of native generated detail. Per-file source sizes, hashes and framing are in `artwork-4k.json`; `tools/export-wallpapers-4k.py` repeats the conversion without restoring deleted files. The Eitr runestone and its motion layers are regenerated natively by their source script. Each wallpaper also has a 21:9 version in `backgrounds-ultrawide/` (3440×1440 JPEG), which monitors at least twice as wide as tall show instead; most are the 21:9 originals the 4K files were cropped from. Their sources are in `artwork-ultrawide.json`, and `tools/export-wallpapers-ultrawide.py` rebuilds them. Batch 04 retains its native sources under `branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-04/`; its `export.py` uses both existing exporters. Ultrawide exports use separate panoramic expansions where needed, otherwise subject-centered crops, without stretched edge padding. Per-image framing lives in batch04 and the ultrawide manifest.

## Next Batch

Number additional original wallpapers after each theme's last one. Do not attach third-party images as references; describe subject, mood, texture and palette in the prompt instead. Keep existing batches in place when adding later images. New wallpapers target 3840×2160 (16:9) and need a 21:9 version in `backgrounds-ultrawide/` (add it to `artwork-ultrawide.json`).

## Reference Rights

Batches 02 and 03 were generated with contact sheets of earlier third-party wallpapers attached as mood references; their `reference` fields name the folders those sheets came from. Those wallpapers are no longer in the repository, and every image that came out too close to them was replaced by batch 04. Each theme's icon, VS Code, Neovim and btop files are generated by `build-theme-files.py` from `integrations.json` and the theme's `colors.toml`; run it (or its `--check`) after changing either.

Theme previews (`preview.png`) show the default wallpaper above a palette strip. Run `python3 tools/theme-previews.py` after changing a theme's default wallpaper or colors. The website uses the same previews for its cards and exports full wallpapers separately.

The Catppuccin additions use owner-requested animated-film atmosphere rather
than the collection’s default Norse screenprint direction. Their exact prompts
and native sources live in each theme’s `references/` directory.

## Eitr batch 09

The owner approved Primordial Drop, Élivágar and Venom Rootlight in painterly
cinematic dark fantasy, an explicit exception to the ink illustration and
no-scenery defaults for this batch. Native owner-approved PNGs are preserved
byte-for-byte; ultrawides are new panoramic compositions from the same final
written prompts, with only Eitr’s emblem used as the drop’s shape reference.
Exact prompts, hashes, sizes and framing: `themes/artwork-batch-09.json`.
Masters and reproducible exports: `branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-09/`.
All batch 09 wallpapers are removed from Eitr; their prompts and native
masters remain as provenance.
Runestone and its live layers are numbered 04.

Eitr, Eitr Light and the ten Store realm themes use local Neovim and VS Code themes from the same contrast-corrected palette roles. Run `python3 themes/build-vscode-themes.py` and its `--check` after palette changes; VSIX archives use fixed timestamps and content-derived versions. The Store generates the realm VSIX archives on installation.

## Eitr Light batch 10

Eitr Light is the pale Primordial Venom counterpart, with green-tinted neutrals,
venom yellow-green accents and jade/emerald secondary roles. Batch 10 recomposes
Primordial Drop, Élivágar and Venom Rootlight on pale mist, bone and parchment
greens, in the painterly cinematic style explicitly requested by the owner.
All six compositions use written briefs only, with no reference images, logos
or lettering. Exact prompts, native sizes and hashes: `artwork-batch-10.json`.
Masters and the reproducible JPEG exporter live in
`branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-10/`. The 3840×2160 and 3440×1440 exports do
not imply native 4K detail. The optional Runestone is omitted because the
existing renderer embeds rune lettering and a logo and has no compatible light
mode. Eitr remains the default theme.

## Eitr and Eitr Light — Batch 12

Eitr now starts with Aurora Fjord, then Dusk Fjord, Rune Seal and the animated
Runestone. Eitr Light starts with the same Dusk Fjord, then its Gates of
Yggdrasil, Rune Seal and Primordial Drop. Eitr's dark Gates and Primordial Drop
are removed; batch 11 retains only three active pairs of native masters.
The owner deliberately approved scenic fjords in a flat ink cel-shaded vector
direction: bold black contours, angular faceted cliffs, triangular pines,
limited colour planes and calm horizontal water reflections. A small longship
anchors each scene; venom-green aurora leads Eitr and warm sunset leads Eitr Light.
This owner-approved scenic direction extends Eitr's default art direction.

`themes/artwork-batch-12.json` preserves both supplied prompts verbatim, input
filenames, native dimensions, hashes and subject-centred framing. The third-party
`ref-fjord-style.png` guided style only and is not distributed. Four native PNGs
and `export.py` live under `branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-12/`. Separate
generated sideways extensions provide the panoramas. Shared Lanczos exports
are 3840×2160 and 3440×1440, without stretching or padding; not native 4K detail.
Website cards use each theme's first wallpaper.
