# Wallpaper Collection

The collection spans 25 themes. The Norse theme combines procedural and generated illustration (see themes/norse/artwork.md). Batches 01-03 originally generated 95 images and the Witcher batch four more, 99 in all. The owner's curation removes and reorders wallpapers; wallpaper names remain stable during review, and manifest entries for removed wallpapers are marked removed with their prompts kept as history.

At the end of Batch 03, Batch 02's former 03 images were promoted to 01, Batch 01's original pair became 02 and 03, and Batch 03 occupied 04 and 05. Later curation changed that order. One Dark Pro (named Witcher for a time) keeps its two owner-supplied wallpapers; its Witcher fan art was removed before public distribution. Historical manifest entries for archived images include an explicit file path.

## Files

- `<theme>/backgrounds/`: installed wallpapers; 3840 x 2160 PNG/JPEG exports.
- `<theme>/artwork.md`: theme-specific prompts and continuation notes.
- `artwork-batch.json`: collection manifest and generation/edit prompts.
- `artwork-batch-02.json`: completed reference-led batch and its generation prompts.
- `artwork-batch-04.json`: owner-approved fresh written-brief replacements, native sources and exact prompts; no image references.
- `artwork-batch-05.json`: the ten approved realm illustrations and their native sources, exact prompts and preview selections.
- `artwork-batch-06.json`: the ten second realm wallpapers, using owner-requested scenic painterly environments, with native sources, exact prompts and framing.
- `artwork-batch-07.json`: realm wallpapers with individually recorded rendering styles, written prompts, native sources and framing; Ragnarök excluded.
- `artwork-batch-03.json`: second reference-led round, two artworks per theme, generated with the built-in image tool.
- `artwork-witcher.json`: the four Witcher fan-art wallpapers once made for One Dark Pro, all removed, with their prompts kept as history.
- `one-dark-pro/references/previous-generated/`: four preserved images generated for One Dark Pro before the Witcher batch.

Renamed theme folders include afterglow, daylight, graphite, jade, moonveil, moss, obsidian, stillwater, and wisteria. Moonveil and Obsidian were removed at the owner’s request; its generation prompts remain marked removed in the historical batch manifests. One Dark Pro was renamed to Witcher and back. Established palette names remain unchanged. External editor plugin identifiers are upstream names and must not be renamed as local folder names.

## Resolution

The shipped collection targets 3840×2160 (16:9). Existing raster artwork is cropped to fill while preserving its main subject, then Lanczos-resampled. These are 4K exports rather than claims of native generated detail. Per-file source sizes, hashes and framing are in `artwork-4k.json`; `tools/export-wallpapers-4k.py` repeats the conversion without restoring deleted files. The Norse runestone and its motion layers are regenerated natively by their source script. Each wallpaper also has a 21:9 version in `backgrounds-ultrawide/` (3440×1440 JPEG), which monitors at least twice as wide as tall show instead; most are the 21:9 originals the 4K files were cropped from. Their sources are in `artwork-ultrawide.json`, and `tools/export-wallpapers-ultrawide.py` rebuilds them. Batch 04 retains its native sources under `branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-04/`; its `export.py` uses both existing exporters and rebuilds its theme previews. Ultrawide exports use separate panoramic expansions where needed, otherwise subject-centered crops, without stretched edge padding. Per-image framing lives in batch04 and the ultrawide manifest.

Keep existing batches in place when adding later images. New wallpapers target 3840×2160 (16:9) and need a 21:9 version in `backgrounds-ultrawide/` (add it to `artwork-ultrawide.json`).

## Reference Rights

Batches 02 and 03 were generated with contact sheets of earlier third-party wallpapers attached as mood references; their `reference` fields name the folders those sheets came from. Those wallpapers are no longer in the repository, and every image that came out too close to them was replaced by batch 04. Each theme's icon, VS Code, Neovim and btop files are generated by `build-theme-files.py` from `integrations.json` and the theme's `colors.toml`; run it (or its `--check`) after changing either.

Batch 04 replaces all selected reference-led images that were too close to earlier artwork with fresh illustrations generated from written briefs only: three each for Stillwater, Afterglow and Moss, and one each for Catppuccin Latte, Rose Pine and Wisteria. Their earlier prompts remain marked removed in batches 02/03. Afterglow and Moss retain their owner-requested soft painterly and chiaroscuro rendering, respectively, with new Norse subjects.

Batch 04’s theme previews are resized copies of its approved artwork. Other theme screenshot previews and Dark/Light category images remain unchanged.

The Catppuccin additions use owner-requested animated-film atmosphere rather
than the collection’s default Norse screenprint direction. Their exact prompts
and native sources live in each theme’s `references/` directory.

## Realm themes — Batch 05

Alfheim, Asgard, Helheim, Jotunheim, Midgard, Muspelheim, Niflheim, Ragnarök, Svartalfheim and Vanaheim are dark themes. Palette sources, the three derived palettes and integration bases are documented in each theme’s `artwork.md`. `artwork-batch-05.json` retains the exact written prompts, native source hashes and crop framing. Native sources and `export.py` live in `branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-05/`; the script rebuilds both exports, their manifest entries and previews. No reference images were used.

Theme menus discover folders and sort their names automatically. The artwork package globs and desktop-package removal rules already cover both wallpaper folders and previews for every theme. The website generates `src/data/themes.json` and its counts from these same folders at build time.

## Realm environments — Batch 06

At the owner's request, these ten second realm wallpapers use scenic landscapes
and rich painterly concept-art lighting instead of the default ink screenprint
style and no-scenery rule. This exception applies only to the ten Batch 06
images, in both aspect ratios. The environments follow each theme's
`colors.toml`, with calm space at the top for the desktop bar.

`artwork-batch-06.json` records exact written briefs, source dimensions, hashes
and framing. No reference images were supplied. All architecture and compositions
are original, without characters, readable lettering or third-party designs.
Native masters and `export.py` live in
`branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-06/`. The exporter updates the 4K and ultrawide
manifests without rebuilding previews. Separate panoramic compositions preserve
landmarks where a crop would lose them; exports never stretch or pad images.

## Realm paintings and illustrations — Batch 07

The owner explicitly requested scenic backgrounds and varied rendering media
for additional realm wallpapers, excluding Ragnarök. This batch-specific exception includes oil painting, gouache, pastel
matte painting, cel shading, loose brushwork, graphic novel, grey wash and
flat poster treatments, with realistic rendering only where requested.
The owner then curated every realm: each keeps two to four wallpapers in a
chosen order, the first being its preview. Wallpapers left out are marked
removed in their batch manifest with their prompts kept, and their exports and
native sources are deleted.

`artwork-batch-07.json` records each rendering style, exact written prompt,
native source dimensions and hashes, and crop offsets. No reference images
were used. Original compositions and characters contain no readable lettering,
logos or third-party likenesses. Sources and `export.py` live under
`branding/sources/wallpapers/batch-07/`; exports are 3840×2160 and 3440×1440
JPEGs, resampled from the recorded native sizes rather than native 4K detail.
Seven separately composed panoramic masters preserve subjects where a crop
would lose them; other ultrawides use recorded subject-preserving crops.
The soul gate uses its panoramic master for both exports. No stretching or
padding is used. Selected artwork has passed agent visual inspection and
remains available for owner review.
