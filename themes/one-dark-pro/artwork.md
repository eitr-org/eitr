# One Dark Pro Artwork

## Current 4K collection

Shipped wallpapers use 3840×2160 (16:9). Existing raster art is cropped to
keep its main subject and Lanczos-resampled; this is not native 4K generation.
Earlier resolution notes below describe the original generation, not the current
exports. See `themes/artwork-4k.json` and `tools/export-wallpapers-4k.py`.

- `backgrounds/01-neon-summit.jpg`
- `backgrounds/02-moonlit-keep.jpg`


The One Dark Pro theme keeps its One Dark palette and upstream editor theme identifiers.

Default: backgrounds/01-neon-summit.jpg.

01 and 02 were supplied by the owner, who states they were generated with Codex. The theme's two Witcher fan-art wallpapers were removed because franchise characters and iconography are not cleared for redistribution; their prompts remain in themes/artwork-witcher.json.

Earlier generated wallpapers and their prompt notes are retained in references/previous-generated, outside wallpaper pickers.
